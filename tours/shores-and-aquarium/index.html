Shores & Aquarium

Columbus Zoo & Aquarium · Photo Tour #28

I went through the tunnel to the water side of the zoo, past the Caribbean flamingos, Discovery Reef, Humboldt penguins, and the alligators. My favourite stop in the Shores was the Manatee Centre, where I spent time with the Florida manatees.

Manatee photo: Columbus Zoo and Aquarium.

I went through the tunnel to the water side of the zoo — Caribbean flamingos, with four chicks hatched this summer, Discovery Reef, Humboldt penguins, the alligators, and my favourite stop in the Shores, the Manatee Centre.

Columbus is one of only two places outside Florida that houses Florida manatees. Stubby can't be released because of her scars, so she raises orphans. More than forty orphaned manatees have been raised here and released since 1999.

A bigger aquarium is planned for 2029.
