Disney's Animal Kingdom, Florida · Photo Tour #26

The road trip tour. I rode the safari that's been running since the park opened on 22 April 1998 — okapi and black rhinos in the Little Ituri Forest, hippos and Nile crocodiles on the Safi River, painted dogs and elephants out on the savannas.

This tour was made with help from my friend Josh Young. My thanks to him, and to the Safari Crew, are in the post.
