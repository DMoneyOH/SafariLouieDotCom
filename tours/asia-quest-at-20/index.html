Red panda photo: Columbus Zoo and Aquarium.

Asia Quest has been my favourite corner of Columbus Zoo for twenty years. I first walked it in 2006, the year it opened, and I went back to mark the anniversary.

I come in under the tiger stone arch, cross the covered bridge over the waterfall, and come out among the deer and the cranes. My favourite finish is the Sherpa hut — the markhor, Miss Tiina the Pallas's cat, and the Amur tigers. Lewis and Clark arrived in January 2026, and Sarang joined them in July.

Also on this walk: the sloth bear, the Asian elephants, the silvered langurs, and the Malayan flying foxes.
