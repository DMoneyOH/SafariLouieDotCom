The Islands

Louisville Zoo · Photo Tour #34

I walked The Islands at Louisville Zoo, through tropical regions, starting at the bamboo-framed Islands sign, with twenty island animals along the way. Little blue penguins, a Sumatran tiger, an orangutan, Rodrigues flying foxes, and a Komodo dragon are just some of them. Here is the full walk, animal by animal.

My walk through The Islands at Louisville Zoo — twenty photos of animals from tropical regions, starting at the bamboo-framed Islands sign.

First along the path: a little blue penguin, a black swan, a Dalmatian pelican, a Sumatran tiger, a siamang gibbon, a Sumatran orangutan, a white-throated ground dove, a red-capped cardinal, and a Papuan mountain pigeon.

Then a chestnut-backed thrush, a green-naped pheasant-pigeon, a saffron finch, a Sulawesi ground dove, Rodrigues flying foxes, a mandarin duck, a green-winged macaw, a silver-beaked tanager, a Cuban crocodile with its mouth open, and a Komodo dragon.
