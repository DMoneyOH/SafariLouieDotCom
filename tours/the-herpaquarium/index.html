The HerpAquarium

Louisville Zoo · Photo Tour #35

I went into the HerpAquarium at Louisville Zoo, under the big green frog, lizard, and snake signage, for eighteen close-ups of reptiles and amphibians. A panther chameleon, an albino alligator, a Gaboon viper, and frogs, geckos, and snakes all the way through. Here they are, first half and second half.

The HerpAquarium at Louisville Zoo, under its big green frog, lizard and snake signage — eighteen close-ups inside.

First half: a colourful panther chameleon among the foliage, an egg-eating snake coiled around speckled eggs, a bright green Amazon horned frog, a camouflaged Malagasy leaf-tailed gecko, a large pale albino American alligator, a Gaboon viper, a gray tree frog, and a banded Pueblan milk snake.

Second half: a New Caledonian giant gecko on bark, a snouted cobra, an eastern indigo snake, a gopher tortoise facing forward, a sandfish skink on the sand, a coiled tiger rattlesnake, an Iraqi eyelid gecko, an African golden skink half-buried in sand, and a Sonoran desert toad.
