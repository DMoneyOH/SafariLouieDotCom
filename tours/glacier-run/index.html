Glacier Run

Louisville Zoo · Photo Tour #37

Glacier Run at Louisville Zoo is the polar bear haven, a whole town reclaimed by wildlife, and it is one of my favourite places to walk. Polar and grizzly bears, a grey seal, sea lions, and a snowy owl all live here, with a story about climate change woven through it. Come along for the walk, starting at the entrance sign.

Glacier Run at Louisville Zoo — the Town of Glacier Run, billed as the Polar Bear Haven of the World and a Sea Lion Watcher's Paradise, with signage for Polar Bears International. The town is a story as much as a habitat: an abandoned Canadian town reclaimed by wildlife, told to highlight climate change.

Past the entrance sign, the residents: a polar bear up close, a grizzly bear resting, a grey seal underwater, a California sea lion swimming, a Baer's pochard duck, a red-breasted goose foraging, a Steller's sea eagle behind the mesh, and a snowy owl perched indoors.
