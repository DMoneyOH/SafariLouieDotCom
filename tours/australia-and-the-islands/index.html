Australia & The Islands

Columbus Zoo & Aquarium · Photo Tour #31

The Voyage to Australia and the Islands at Columbus Zoo, in at the wooden entrance sign.

The Australian side: a Queensland koala, a Tasmanian devil, a sulphur-crested cockatoo, an eastern grey kangaroo, and rainbow and coconut lorikeets.

The Islands side: a Bornean orangutan, a white-handed lar gibbon, a siamang gibbon hanging, an Asian small-clawed otter, a yellow-headed temple turtle underwater, a painted stork on one leg, a black swan, two Dalmatian pelicans beak to beak, an Indian crested porcupine, and a Komodo dragon.
