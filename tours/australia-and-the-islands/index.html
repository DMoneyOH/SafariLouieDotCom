Australia & The Islands

Columbus Zoo & Aquarium · Photo Tour #31

I walked through the Voyage to Australia and the Islands at Columbus Zoo, starting at the wooden entrance sign. On the Australian side I met a Queensland koala, a Tasmanian devil, and an eastern grey kangaroo, then crossed to the Islands side for an orangutan, gibbons, and a Komodo dragon.

I started at the wooden entrance sign for the Voyage to Australia and the Islands at Columbus Zoo.

On the Australian side, I saw a Queensland koala, a Tasmanian devil, a sulphur-crested cockatoo, an eastern grey kangaroo, and rainbow and coconut lorikeets.

On the Islands side, I saw a Bornean orangutan, a white-handed lar gibbon, a siamang gibbon hanging, an Asian small-clawed otter, a yellow-headed temple turtle underwater, a painted stork standing on one leg, a black swan, two Dalmatian pelicans beak to beak, an Indian crested porcupine, and a Komodo dragon.
