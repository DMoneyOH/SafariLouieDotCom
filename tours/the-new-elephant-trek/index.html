The NEW Elephant Trek

Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden · Photo Tour #23

Elephant photo: Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden.

Built on the old Safari parking lot and opened in phases across 2024 and spring 2025, Elephant Trek is now the best Asian elephant complex I have ever seen. The elephants went from 25,000 square feet to nearly three and a half acres.

Siamang Point came with phase two — gibbons Sebastian and Berback, rhinoceros hornbills with room to actually fly, and Asian small-clawed otters Munti and Flounder, parents of litters in May 2025 and March 2026.
