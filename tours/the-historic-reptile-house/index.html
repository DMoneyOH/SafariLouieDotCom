The Historic Reptile House

Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden · Photo Tour #16

In 1875, the year the Cincinnati Zoo opened, one of its first exhibits was the Monkey House. In 1953 the reptiles moved into it, from their former building — the building that Wings of the World occupies now.

Over seventy years later, the historic house is still home to the zoo's reptiles, and the tour walks it the way generations of visitors have.
