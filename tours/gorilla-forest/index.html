Gorilla Forest

Columbus Zoo & Aquarium · Photo Tour #36

This was a short stop under the Gorilla Forest sign, and it was worth slowing down for. A pygmy hippo, and a western lowland gorilla behind the mesh, looking straight at the camera. This habitat rewards a slow walk, so stand a while and let them come to you.

Gorilla photo: Columbus Zoo and Aquarium. Pygmy hippo photo: Metro Richmond Zoo.
