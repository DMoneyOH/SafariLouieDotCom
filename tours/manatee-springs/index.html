Manatee Springs

Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden · Photo Tour #20

This building was a standard aquarium for nearly 50 years, but when a fire destroyed the progress of the aquarium's renovation, the zoo turned the aquatic life theme to another level in 1999 — Manatee Springs.

You come in through the atrium, which feels like you're actually in Florida, past an American alligator and an American crocodile, then the river of grass, with a green basilisk. The star habitat holds alligator gars, and their friends, the Florida manatees.

Then the alien invaders tank, which teaches why invasive species are such a huge problem, a wall of Florida's biodiversity, and the palmetto scrub, with an eastern diamondback rattlesnake and a gray rat snake. There's a full skeleton of a manatee to look at, too — and a strong message about Florida's future, and why animal conservation matters. One of the best Florida exhibits ever built.
