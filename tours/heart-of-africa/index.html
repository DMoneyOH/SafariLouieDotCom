Heart of Africa

Columbus Zoo & Aquarium · Photo Tour #27

Giraffe photo: Columbus Zoo and Aquarium.

Forty-three acres, opened in 2014, and some of the best habitats in the country. Lions Imara, Nyasi and Roary up front, and behind them in the same view, the giraffes and zebras out across the grass.

The camels' old ground is now home to Aldabra tortoises and miniature donkeys. The Watering Hole rotates hyenas and warthogs. And the giraffe herd keeps growing — the newest Masai calf, Jasper, was born in March 2026, the fifth calf since 2020.
