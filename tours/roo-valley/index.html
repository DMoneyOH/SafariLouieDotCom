Roo Valley

Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden · Photo Tour #15

When the Queen City zoo opened back in 1875, this steep hill started with two enclosures for elk and white-tailed deer. The deer line followed in 1942, then Wildlife Canyon — the last home of the camels, takins and warthogs, and the last place in the Western Hemisphere that the Sumatran rhino called home.

As part of the More Home to Roam master plan, Roo Valley opened in August of 2020. Through the double doors into the Outback, you immediately share your space with the valley's red and western grey kangaroos, with their sand pits, trees and fans to keep them cool.

Past their barn, another set of doors takes you to the coast — the largest colony of little blue penguins in America, and personally my favourite blue penguin habitat. The penguins share their space with three species of ducks: freckled, New Zealand scaup and spotted whistling ducks.
